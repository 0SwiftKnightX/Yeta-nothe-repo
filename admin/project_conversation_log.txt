======================================================================

Punches, kicks, elbows, knees, palm strikes, footwork, stances, blocks, parries, slips, dodges, counters, combos, clinches, grapples, throws, takedowns, submissions, martial arts and fictional styles.

B. Armed melee:
Swords, daggers, staffs, axes, spears, polearms, shields, dual wielding, improvised weapons, disarms, and transitions between weapon and unarmed combat.

C. Firearms and ranged combat:
Pistols, rifles, shotguns, precision weapons, energy weapons and other selected ranged systems; aiming, ammunition, reloads, movement, range, cover, and authoritative hit validation.

D. Magic and supernatural abilities:
Spells, elemental powers, energy projection, barriers, enchantments, summons, healing, transformations, and hybrids.

XR target: Meta Quest 3S, standalone Android XR/ARM64, Godot 4.7.2 stable and OpenXR as the intended baseline. Head and hands are the core tracked inputs. A virtual lower body is needed, but head-and-hand tracking alone cannot be represented as accurate real foot tracking. Estimated/animated lower-body movement must not be described as actual foot tracking.

5. FIGHTER DNA AND PROGRESSION
======================================================================

Fighter DNA is inspired by the “Driver DNA” concept discussed in connection with Juiced 2: Hot Import Nights, but is to be an original evolving multidimensional behavioral identity based on actual play. It is not merely a class, skill tree, personality quiz, or generic stat sheet.

It may record behavior, preferences, proficiency, context, outcomes, confidence and history to influence training, rivals, NPC reactions, signature combinations and discovery. Preference must be distinguished from proficiency. Missing telemetry must not be interpreted as evidence that a behavior never occurred.

The user specified candidate pools named “Slang,” “Wild,” and “Cold.” Do not silently change those names; their exact meanings and effects remain open questions.

Skill definitions should be distinct from individual character mastery. A player can teach a permitted skill definition to another player; the learner may master it and branch it differently. Branches can coexist, and players may later learn one another’s variants subject to explicit rules.

6. COMPOSABLE SKILL SYSTEM
======================================================================

The user wants a reusable library of templates/components such as:
- SkillTemplate
- ElementalOrbBase
- MeleeWeaponBase
- WeaponAction
- ProjectileEmission
- ElementalPayload
- PassiveModifier
- ActivationCondition

Skills can combine procedures, templates, elements, passives, equipment, gestures, distance, resources, cooldowns and context conditions. A component library should allow valid combinations without requiring developers to manually author every combination. Combinations must be type-checked, versioned, bounded, and tested.

Example: a skill begins as a sword action with a horizontal slash. A compatible elemental effect may be added so a valid sword swing emits an orb in the direction of the swing. Activation may require a compatible sword, valid swing, stance, distance, available resources and cooldown readiness. If the target is too close, the normal slash may still happen while the orb fails its distance condition. A firearm does not satisfy a sword requirement unless the definition explicitly permits it.

Materials/crystals can be used to create or finalize a skill scroll or similar artifact. “Inscripted” was proposed by the user as a possible name for the finalization/sealing step; it is not yet approved.

Lifecycle:
Compose → Resource → Inscript/Finalize → Learn → Train → Verify → Master/Evolve → Teach/Branch.

Training may use dummies and measurable conditions such as target distance, action type, timing, stance and successful activation. Passive abilities can use templates, crystals, elements and other components. Potential modifiers include damage, number of strikes, cost, bullets, ricochets and other effects, all bounded by balance and performance rules.

- Fictional in-world command lines do not grant arbitrary real server, operating-system, filesystem, secret, or real-network access.

- Live additions must not corrupt active worlds or break old content. Use stable IDs, versions, dependency declarations, migration rules, staged release and rollback.

8. OPERATOR / PET / DIGITAL PET / LIVING FAMILIAR
======================================================================

These are separate but complementary systems.

1. Operator / digital operative:
A persistent digital partner accessed through a PET, with identity, personality, history and abilities. It can assist with in-game network-domain missions and later accompany the player in the physical game world. Network operations must be confined to simulated or explicitly authorized in-game domains.

2. Digital pet:
A virtual creature cared for and trained by the operator. Branching evolution, duplication and fusion with compatible digital pets may be possible under explicit rules.

3. Living familiar:
A creature that may begin as an egg, hatchling, primordial warp crystal, void creature or time-capsuled dinosaur egg. Species-specific awakening conditions may use several parameters. Care, feeding, training, combat exposure, learning and bonding matter. It should not die just because the player is absent; define self-care and food-seeking. It should remain loyal and not abandon/betray its owner absent explicit design rules. Other players cannot ordinarily claim it; transfers require controlled mechanisms.

Life stages may include baby/hatchling, child, adolescent/teen and adult, varying by species. Some species may be rideable at suitable stages; adult status does not mean every species is rideable. Candidate stats include knowledge, perception, dexterity, agility, endurance, luck and charisma, distinct from mastery, experience and teamwork. Tamer ranks are placeholders: Pet Tamer → Beast Handler → Beastmaster → Master Beastmaster.

PET hardware generations are separate from operator progression and unlocked services. Early devices provide basic communication; later generations can unlock network navigation, security analysis and coordination, while older devices remain useful.

“Augmented User Fusion” is the user’s term for fusing player and operator into a unified combat form: the player directly controls the avatar while operator capabilities augment combat.

Operator-compatible suit/body is a distinct system inspired by modular robotic bodies: head, torso, arms, legs, armor, mobility and weapon modules. PET, operator, digital core/chip and physical body are four distinct concepts. Body quality is separate from operator progression. Deployment must be location- and permission-validated.
